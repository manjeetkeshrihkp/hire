Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Information We Collect

When you apply to Elitex Media, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, Gmail address, phone number, location, gender, language, experience, skills, and academic completion details.

How We Use Your Information

  • To review and process your application.
  • To contact you about opportunities, interviews, and application updates.
  • To improve our recruitment process and applicant experience.

Google Sign-In

If you choose Fetch with Google, we receive the Gmail address associated with your Google account. We use it to verify your email and connect it with your application. We do not receive your Google password.

Data Storage and Security

Application information is stored using Firebase services. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share application details only with authorized Elitex Media team members who need the information to review your application.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your application information by contacting the Elitex Media team.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Elitex Media team through the contact details provided with your application opportunity.
